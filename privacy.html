Hello Flow is an offline arcade game made by Dodobob.

We do not collect, store or share any personal data. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases.

  • Game progress (scores, grades, badges, settings) is saved only on your device. It is never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it.
  • Permissions: the game does not use the camera, microphone, photos, location or contacts.
  • Children: the game is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including children.
  • Links: the game does not open outside links in this version.

If a future version adds Game Center leaderboards, Apple handles that data under Apple's own privacy policy, and this page will be updated before that version ships.
